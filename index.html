Section 01

Skills & Qualifications

Technical competencies and academic benchmarks required to thrive in the music industry.

Instrumental Proficiency

Exceptional technique, tone control, and dexterity on primary and secondary instruments. Ability to adapt tone across genre styles (Jazz, Rock, Classical, Pop).

  • Multi-instrumental versatility
  • Improvisational competence

Theory & Sight-Reading

Fluent reading of standard notation, lead sheets, and chord charts under high-pressure sight-reading conditions for television, film, or theatre sessions.

  • ABRSM Grade 8 Theory level
  • Ear training & transposing

Studio & DAW Production

Command of Digital Audio Workstations (Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live). Understanding micro-timing, click tracks, MIDI orchestration, and home studio tracking.

  • Pro Tools / Logic Pro X mastery
  • Remote recording capabilities

Performance Stamina

Physical endurance and mental focus to maintain peak physical execution during demanding multi-set touring schedules, theatrical pit work, and long rehearsals.

  • Ergonomic injury prevention
  • High pressure stage composure

Formal Qualifications

Recognized credentials including Music Degrees (BMus / BA Hons), Conservatoire Diplomas, and Grade 8 certifications from ABRSM, Trinity, or Rockschool.

  • BMus / Conservatoire Degree
  • Rockschool / ABRSM Grade 8

Collaboration & Direction

Interpersonal communication within large ensembles, working seamlessly under musical directors (MDs), accepting real-time critique, and supporting bandmates.

  • Following Musical Directors (MD)
  • Tight rhythmic ensemble playing
Section 02

Core Musician Duties

Daily tasks, performance responsibilities, and professional expectations.

01

Rehearsal & Technical Preparation

Practicing repertory independently before rehearsals. Attending band sound checks, sectionals, and full ensemble dry runs to polish arrangements and dynamics.

02

Live Performance & Gigging

Performing live concerts, festivals, corporate galas, theatrical shows, and private engagements. Delivering captivating stage presence with faultless timing.

03

Session Recording

Tracking instrumental parts in commercial recording studios for artists, producers, film scores, TV syncs, and advertising commercials.

04

Song Composing & Arranging

Writing original hooks, orchestrating horn/string sections, creating lead sheets, and adapting existing songs for custom live ensemble setups.

05

Equipment Setup & Maintenance

Maintaining, tuning, and safely transporting delicate gear. Setting up amps, pedalboards, wireless monitors, and PA equipment securely.

06

Self-Promotion & Networking

Managing online portfolios, social media engagement, liaising with booking agents, negotiating performance contracts, and managing tax filings.

Section 03

Average Pay & Rates

UK salary benchmarks and Musicians' Union (MU) recommended minimum rates.

Full-Time / Salaried

£26,000 - £48,000+

Per Annum (UK Benchmark)

Typical for full-time orchestra members, resident West End pit musicians, military band performers, and tenured academic music teachers.

Freelance Live Gigs

£150 - £350+

Per Gig / Engagement

Standard pubs, functions, weddings, and corporate events. MU recommended minimum live performance rate starts around £140-£180+ plus travel allowances.

Studio Recording

£180 - £400+

Per 3-Hour Session

Commercial studio session recording rates aligned with MU union session standards, incorporating broadcast overdub fees and sync royalties.
